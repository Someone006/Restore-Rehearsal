# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for people with different backgrounds and
levels of operational experience.

Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss technical choices rather than people, respect privacy,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or publishing another person's
private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior undermines a safe collaborative environment.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's moderation or private reporting tools.
Do not include private information in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports as
confidentially and fairly as possible.
