# UX Flows and Product Content

Lens ID: `ux-flow`

Review routes/screens, forms, navigation, product copy, destructive actions, onboarding, and journey
test evidence. Look for unverified loading, empty, validation, error, recovery, permission, and undo
states. Source presence alone cannot establish usability.

Propose task-based scenarios and observable acceptance criteria. Require user research or human
review before making subjective product conclusions; do not optimize a visual graph at the expense
of the real user flow.
