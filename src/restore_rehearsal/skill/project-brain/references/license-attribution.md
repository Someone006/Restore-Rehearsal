# License and Attribution

Lens ID: `license-attribution`

Check for project license/notice artifacts, resolved dependency inventories, vendored material,
release contents, SBOMs, and attribution evidence. The
[Apache Licensing FAQ](https://www.apache.org/foundation/license-faq.html) confirms that
Apache-licensed software may be used commercially, subject to its terms.

Flag exact artifacts and dependencies for review; do not decide license compatibility, source-offer
obligations, patent risk, or jurisdiction-specific requirements. This lens is not legal advice.
