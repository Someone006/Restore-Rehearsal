# Supply-chain evidence

Generate checksums and a standards-based SBOM for the exact artifacts. Review direct and transitive
licenses, vulnerabilities with context, action pinning, least-privilege workflow permissions,
provenance subject hashes, signature identity, and revocation/incident procedures.

An SBOM, scanner, signature, or attestation is one evidence source—not proof of safety or legal
compliance. Never sign a different artifact than the one tested.
